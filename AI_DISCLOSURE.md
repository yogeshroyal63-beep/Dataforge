## What was verified by humans

Every technical claim in the app was verified against the primary source papers before inclusion. Specifically:

- The linear-attention recurrence (Katharopoulos et al., 2020) was traced to the original paper's Equation 5 and implemented independently in `src/models/linearAttention.js`.
- BDH and BDH-CQ claims (Kosowski et al., 2025; Engdahl et al., 2026) were verified against the arXiv preprints. No claim about BDH or BDH-CQ was generated by an AI model and used without cross-checking against the primary source.
- The 29.5% pass@2 ARC-AGI-1 and $0.00070/task figures are taken directly from Engdahl et al. (2026) and are labeled as developer-reported results, not independent evaluations.
- Benchmark results shown on the Experiment page are computed live by the visitor's browser using `src/models/benchmark.js`. These numbers are reproducible from source (see README).

## What AI did not do

- AI tools did not write or verify any mathematical derivation or equation.
- AI tools did not determine which papers to cite or what claims to attribute to them.
- No AI-generated text was submitted as a technical claim without human verification.
- The toy model implementations (`fullAttention.js`, `linearAttention.js`, `toyEncoding.js`, `benchmark.js`) were written and reviewed by team members and can be explained and defended in full.

All team members understand and can explain every major component of this submission.
